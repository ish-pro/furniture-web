OUR STORY

Crafting Spaces With Purpose & Soul.

Founded in 2022, FURNIQA was born out of a simple belief: that your home should be your sanctuary. We partner with master artisans to create timeless furniture that honors natural materials and sustainable design.

Minimalism Meets Uncompromising Quality.

Every sofa, table, and lighting fixture in our collection undergoes rigorous testing to ensure it stands up to everyday family living while maintaining gallery-worthy elegance.

We use 100% ethically sourced timbers, organic fabrics, and non-toxic finishes because we believe beauty should never come at the planet's expense.
